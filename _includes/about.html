02 —About

Rebecca Goldberg

I’m passionate about creating opportunities for young people to lead and thrive — which means addressing structural barriers and leveraging existing and untapped opportunities to invest in young people’s well-being.

I spent seven years in philanthropy leading a national youth-development strategy as part of the S.D. Bechtel, Jr. Foundation spend-down. That work surfaced how essential organizational capacity, data-informed decisions, and addressing field-wide inequities are — especially when organizations are navigating uncertainty. I previously led nonprofit initiatives focused on creating equitable learning environments through youth-development and career-pathway programs.

I believe the best work happens through collaboration, trust, and strong relationships — whether you’re working directly with young people, with schools and nonprofits, or with philanthropy.
